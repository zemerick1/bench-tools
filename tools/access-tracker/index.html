FAQ

Where does this export even come from?

ClearPass: Monitoring → Access Tracker, open a session, export / download dashboard details. You get DashboardDetails.zip with a tmp/<session-id>/ folder full of the good stuff (and some of the boring stuff).

Why can’t I just drop the zip?

Because zip-in-the-browser means a dependency, and this toolbox is allergic to “just one more library.” Double-click extract works on every OS your ticket queue has ever invented. Folder + multi-file covers the same ground without the gym membership.

My browser yelled about uploading a folder?

That’s the browser, not a toast from us. Choosing a folder means “this page can read the files inside,” so Chrome/Edge/etc. double- check with you. We only look for the Access Tracker trio; we don’t ship anything anywhere. Hit allow, or use Choose files and pick Dashboard_Details.txt by hand.

What if I only have Dashboard_Details.txt?

Still useful — result, identity, MAC, SSID, service, roles, and the Alerts reject reason when it’s a fail. Service config explains why that service matched. Request logs are the director’s cut when CPPM bothered to write one.

Does this call ClearPass? The cloud? My mom?

Nope. Parsing is all client-side. Refresh and it’s gone. No CoA, no API, no password archaeology — and secrets that slip through get redacted like a bad sitcom.

RADIUS and TACACS?

Both show up in Access Tracker. We handle the usual wireless/wired RADIUS fields and the TACACS summary shape (the labels are a little different; life is chaos). Weird export? Keep a redacted copy — samples make parsers smarter.

RADIUS dictionaries?

Yup — IETF, Aruba, HPE, and Juniper packed into radius-dict.json. So 15 becomes Ethernet and 10 becomes Call-Check instead of staring at you like a Sudoku. Refresh the XMLs under dictionaries/ and run python3 update_radius_dict.py when ClearPass invents new toys.

What we won’t invent

Full packet hex fanfic, live CoA, or a root cause when the logs shrugged. Empty Alerts + empty logs = we tell you that, not a bedtime story.
