Hardware Platform Support

One card per platform across HPE Aruba and HPE Juniper. Aruba keeps correlated firmware tracks (AOS-10 / Instant — first / last support). Juniper EX, QFX, and Mist APs deep-link into Pathfinder with series + lifecycle from the product catalog.

How data is joined: AOS-10 mins come from the New Central supported-devices matrix. Instant (IAP) mins/lasts come from Classic Central’s Supported Instant APs table (same model matched across sources). 700 Series Instant floor of 8.13.3.x is added on top of that table (not yet listed there). Results are grouped by series; expand a card for details.
About datasheets (and our pure intentions). In a perfect world I’d deep-link every model to its shiny official datasheet PDF so you could one-click from “is this parked?” to “how many radios does it even have?” Alas, HPE would rather we not send a fleet of helpful little robots to vacuum their entire documentation empire into a duffel bag. Scraping is discouraged; we heard them; we are behaving (mostly). So this page sticks to a hand-refreshed support snapshot plus release-note links—no clandestine datasheet heist, no “accidentally” mirrored PDF CDN, no trench-coat energy. If you need the glossy one-pager, the product site still has it. We just won’t pretend we nicked it for you.

FAQ

Is this an official HPE support list?

No. This is an unofficial helper — an honest attempt to make firmware compatibility lookups less painful by correlating public docs (AOS-10 matrix, Instant supported APs, and a few known gaps such as 700 Series Instant), plus the public Juniper Pathfinder product catalog for EX / QFX / Mist APs. Tables go stale, pages disagree, and some platforms or trains will be missing or slightly wrong. Always confirm on current HPE / Juniper documentation before production upgrades, purchases, or support cases.

What are the firmware tracks?

AOS-10 — New Central / AOS-10 support matrix (AP, gateway, bridge, AOS-CX / AOS-S). Instant (IAP 8.x) — Instant OS support from the Classic Central Instant AP table (plus 700 Series at 8.13.3.x). Junos / Mist AP — Pathfinder product entries for EX, QFX, and Mist APs (lifecycle + product page; not a full Junos first/last matrix yet). One Aruba model can carry both AOS-10 and Instant tracks; filter by track to see only platforms that support that code.

What does “parked” / EOL mean here?

On Aruba rows, the official matrix lists a Last Supported Release. We label that parked: the platform is still documented, but capped on that train (for example 10.4.x.x). On AOS-10 rows, the release-notes button aims at the newest published notes under that train from the AOS-10 all-releases index. On Juniper rows, Pathfinder’s isEOL flag maps to parked / EOL — confirm lifecycle on the Pathfinder product page.

Where do Juniper platforms come from?

Pulled from Juniper Pathfinder (https://apps.juniper.net/home/). We include EX Series, QFX Series, and Mist Access Points for now. Refresh with python3 update_juniper.py — Aruba rows are left intact.

How do you pick “latest AOS-10” for current platforms?

The RN Home page doesn’t always shout a single version number—it sends you to All Releases. We walk that list, take the highest version (semver-ish), and deep-link there. When you refresh the snapshot, that target moves with HPE’s catalog.

Where does the data come from?

Static snapshots of public HPE / Juniper docs — not a live feed and not complete coverage of every product line. AOS-10 from Supported Devices and the AOS-10 All Releases map; Instant from Classic Central’s supported Instant APs table (plus manual 700 Series Instant notes); Juniper from Pathfinder’s product catalog (EX, QFX, Mist APs). Refresh the AOS-10 slice with python3 update_data.py and Juniper with python3 update_juniper.py (see README). Gaps are expected — treat this as a starting point, not the source of truth.

Why no datasheet links?

Because we asked the universe for “one polite table of support plus PDFs for every SKU,” and the universe (wearing an HPE lanyard) said scraping is a no-no. We’re not mad. We’re just… link-poor. Use the official product pages for datasheets; use this tool for “when did it ship on AOS-10 and is it parked?”

AOS-CX / AOS-S links?

Those platforms use different firmware trains. Rows still show minimum / recommended text from Central’s matrix; AOS-10 release notes buttons apply to AP, gateway, and bridge rows.
